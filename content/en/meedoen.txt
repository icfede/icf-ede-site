Would you like to be part of the mission of ICF Ede? Wonderful! We are a growing community and are grateful for every kind of support. There are many ways to get involved.

@tegel familie

## Come

Come to our meetings! That way we can get to know each other and you can get a taste of the atmosphere. Literally, because after the service we eat together. [See the upcoming meetings](#icf:home#bijeenkomsten).

@tegel veilig

## Pray

- For the spreading of the gospel
- For persecuted Christians and the governments of their countries
- For the many refugees in the world and among us
- For welcome and healing for our displaced brothers and sisters
- For unity and wisdom in dealing with all our differences at ICF Ede
- For cooperation and unity between the churches in Ede

@tegel liefde

## Serve

Do you long to take part? Come and visit, and discover whether ICF Ede is where God wants to give you a place.

You can also help in practical ways. For example, we could use help with:

- Driving brothers and sisters to and from the asylum seekers’ centre (AZC) in Wageningen
- The kitchen team: setting out coffee and tea, warming up and serving meals, and washing up
- The children’s team, helping to make the children’s services a good time of learning

Does any of this appeal to you? Send an email to [contact@icfede.nl](mailto:contact@icfede.nl).

@tegel vrijgevig

## Give

You can also support us financially. We use your gift to rent our venue, to buy Bibles and literature in many languages, and for materials for the children’s ministry.
